# Product Strategy

## Working Title

NHS Capacity Intelligence Dashboard

## Product Problem

Emergency care pressure is difficult to interpret quickly when demand, admissions, and bed occupancy are spread across separate NHS data publications. Product and operational teams need a clearer way to identify when capacity pressure is rising and where attention should be focused.

## Product Thesis

Combining NHS England A&E activity data with bed occupancy data can create a practical early-warning view of emergency care capacity pressure.

## Target Users

- NHS operational managers
- hospital capacity teams
- Integrated Care Board analysts
- public-sector health analysts
- healthtech product teams

## MVP Scope

The first version should answer:

- How high is General and Acute bed occupancy?
- How much A&E demand is being reported?
- How many emergency admissions are occurring?
- Are decision-to-admit waits increasing?
- What overall pressure band does the system appear to be in?

## Product Metrics

- datasets integrated
- months covered
- regions covered where available
- number of operational indicators
- dashboard load time
- pressure score completeness
- user feedback count after publication

## Analytical Metrics

- General and Acute occupancy rate
- General and Acute beds available
- General and Acute beds occupied
- total A&E attendances
- total emergency admissions
- emergency admissions via A&E
- waits over 4 hours from decision to admit
- waits over 12 hours from decision to admit

## Product Value

This project should demonstrate:

- product discovery and problem framing
- data-informed product strategy
- public-sector digital technology awareness
- ability to turn analysis into a usable digital product
- clear communication through research writing
- practical healthtech and data-product execution

## Current Risk

The original notebook only uses the bed occupancy dataset. The A&E dataset must be integrated before the project can credibly claim to combine demand and capacity data.
