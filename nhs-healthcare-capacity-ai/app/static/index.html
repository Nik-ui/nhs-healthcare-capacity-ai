How To Use The AI

Ask one clear operational question at a time. The assistant will retrieve relevant NHS data, use Bedrock to reason over it, and remember useful answers for later follow-up.

1
Ask about current pressure Example: Which region has the highest bed occupancy?
2
Ask about short-term trend Example: What is the likely A&E pressure trend over the next 3 months?
3
Ask follow-up questions Example: What did I ask earlier about capacity pressure?
